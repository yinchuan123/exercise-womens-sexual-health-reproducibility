Figure 1. PRISMA 2020 flow diagram for the exercise-focused synthesis

The focused searches yielded 28,511 records from databases and registers. Deduplication yielded 21,208 record groups, with a further 658 distinct groups from an earlier intervention archive. The resulting 21,866 record groups form the screening frame. Documented routes describe record dispositions. Their chronology relative to report retrieval could not be recovered. Dashed boxes mark unresolved whole-review counts. Separate report catalogue evidence supports 85 principal reports representing 84 study families in the available synthesis, with 114 reports awaiting classification. Appendix S1 provides the record and report accounting and explains the unresolved fields.
