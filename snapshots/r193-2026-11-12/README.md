## Exact accounting

groups: 18,827 record exclusions and 3,097 advanced groups. The record/report
identity conversion is 3,097 - 1 proceedings container + 4 additional reports
- 13 convergent report links = 3,087 distinct reports. Eleven aliases or
containers are retained separately and are not additional reports.

3,087 = 499 not retrieved + 2,588 assessed.
2,588 = 509 own-reason exclusions + 1,701 noncontributing report contexts
+ 48 awaiting classification at the cutoff + 330 included reports.
330 = 191 principal result reports + 139 companion reports.

The 191 principal reports map to 187 conservative reported-randomized strategy
groups. This is an explicit grouping policy, not proof of zero cohort/person
intersection, not 187 pure exercise effects, and not the K of any one synthesis.
Splitting the Yun reports gives 188 as a separate sensitivity count. Whole
programmes and adaptive offers of physical tasks remain explicitly scoped.
The 48 eligibility gaps and 499 unavailable reports are exact cutoff counts;
their missing facts do not become known by author approval. No whole-review
unique female N is asserted. Source denominators and observed/model Ns remain
separate. All fitted clinical comparisons have K=2.

## Reproduce

From the repository root run:

```sh
python3 snapshots/r193-2026-11-12/verify_snapshot.py
Rscript snapshots/r193-2026-11-12/analysis/verify_existing_six_clinical_fits.R
```

The Python replay verifies every new snapshot fingerprint, report partition,
principal/companion links, record/report conversion and existing risk/GRADE
coverage counts. The R replay verifies 74 fields across six existing fits for
three restricted comparisons. It adds no new model or effect. The 22 historical
published-input diagnostic fits stay in their separate diagnostic package.
Sparse post hoc/exploratory fits, outcome-specific bias and certainty limits,
missing effective N and the eight old certainty differences remain explicit.

The portable `rebuild_prisma.py` uses the same current aggregate count file and
layout as the approved merged Figure 1. Run it with Python and ReportLab,
using `--output` to choose a separate rebuilt PDF; it does not overwrite the
approved figure. The source PDF retains its generation-time metadata; the
actual release adoption is recorded in this current snapshot.

Risk records are 166 exact result targets and certainty records are 91
partly overlapping views. These do not imply every outcome in 187 groups has
been rated. Historical source-coverage lists describe their original subset.
No preprint or journal submission is performed or authorized by this release.
Earlier snapshots remain immutable; root CURRENT_SNAPSHOT.json identifies
