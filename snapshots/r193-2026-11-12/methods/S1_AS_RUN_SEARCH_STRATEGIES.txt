Supplement S1 focused search strategies and record accounting

The searches below were executed on 29 October 2026 (China Standard Time). They are the declared focused update streams for the exercise-containing synthesis. The registered broader intervention review and the known-source-informed query refinement are described separately in the dated methods chronology.

The eight platforms contributed 28,511 source export members. Within the current identity projection, 7,303 duplicate representations were removed, leaving 21,208 focused record groups. The targeted archive intake supplied 851 additional representations. Removing 45 duplicates within that intake and 148 overlaps with the focused frame left 658 additional groups. The resulting recorded frame comprised 21,866 canonical record groups. These are counts of record units. The final sequential flow of reports sought, assessed and included is handled separately.
