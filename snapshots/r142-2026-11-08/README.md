# R142 available-evidence count and linkage snapshot

8 November 2026. Three actual author receipt files were verified against the prior
23-question packet. The two named reviewers agreed on 21 questions. Actual third
author actions resolved two differences; the two dissenting choices are retained.
AI recommendations were not substituted for author choices. This publication is
a derived process snapshot, not approval or publication of the revised manuscript.

The record count is 29,490 minus 7,550 duplicates = 21,940 screened records;
18,827 record exclusions plus 3,113 advanced record groups = 21,940. Reconciling two
distinct already-held reports and one duplicated report representation gives
3,114 own reports. Of these, 729 lacked the required report extent (531 indexed
abstracts with journal body unavailable and 198 without own text). The 2,385
available-source assessments partition into 138 report exclusions, 1,316 protocol
or no-current-result carriers, 726 awaiting classification, and 205 included
reports. Assessment sources include documented limited abstracts and registers;
2,385 is not a claim that 2,385 full journal papers were read independently twice.
The protocol and awaiting inventories are not clinical exclusions of parent trials.

The 205 included reports comprise 125 selected result objects and 80 available
exact-parent companion reports. Source-supported conservative counting gives 121
possible-dependency clusters, or 122 if the two unresolved Yun reports are
independent. This policy does not prove the same RCT. The current known female
allocation or preallocation-index-woman subtotal is 14,156 across 118 source-family
groups, with three unknown initial female allocation denominators. An alternative
stricter first-allocation layer excluding 375 LookAHEAD ancillary index women is
13,781 across 117 groups. Neither subtotal is a deduplicated adult female total,
a sexual-outcome denominator, or a whole-review participant total. LILA and Due
add reports but no new parent K or N; the distinct DMT source adds 110 once.

The source-mapping TSVs publish derived report IDs, bibliographic identifiers,
current stages and cluster labels. Stage and cluster labels preserve unknowns.
They do not contain abstracts, original full texts, private notes, author receipts,
communications or the unapproved manuscript. Bibliographic titles are retained
for inspectability; external rights in original publications are unchanged.

Forty-one new current registry-result module uses await 123 actual author choices
in S27; the remaining eighteen of the 59 source checks reuse existing decisions
or retain specific reporting/body-task gates. These proposals are not applied in
this snapshot and do not newly exclude parent trials. No model values, RoB2 or
GRADE ratings were changed. Three existing restricted comparisons and six fits
remain at the historical v0.1.0 commit; 22 diagnostic fits remain a separate audit.
